## 🏥 **Live in production: Cashless Hospital Finder**

**👉 [medibuddy.in/surgery-care/find-hospitals](https://www.medibuddy.in/surgery-care/find-hospitals)**

Patients defer surgery because they can't tell which hospital near them does their procedure
cashless on their insurer. I replaced a lead-capture form that was losing 80% of users with an
interactive finder: pincode or city + treatment + insurer → hospitals ranked by distance, rating
and insurance panel → the doctors there who perform that specific procedure.

| Live impact | |
|---|---|
| **~50** | leads per day |
| **~30** | surgeries booked per month |
| **₹21 lakh** | booked value per month (₹2.1M) |

I built the working prototype myself first, to prove the distance ranking and the
condition-to-speciality matching before committing an engineering sprint.

**🔧 [Open-source prototype](https://github.com/raopunit95/surgery-network-finder)** ·
**▶ [Live demo](https://raopunit95.github.io/surgery-network-finder/)** — 648 hospitals,
3,547 doctors, 218 surgeries, 144 cities, 36 insurers. Static site, zero dependencies.
